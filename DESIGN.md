## Brand & Style

This design system crafts a dependable, energetic, and transparent telecom conversion experience. Built specifically for mobile number portability (*portabilidad*) flows, it balances corporate credibility with conversational warmth and decisive urgency.

### Aesthetic Movement & Tone
- **Style:** Corporate Modern infused with Clean Tech simplicity. It blends crisp white and cool slate foundations with bold brand hues and confident, tactile call-to-actions.
- **Personality:** Trustworthy, dynamic, frictionless, and customer-first. The user interface prioritizes clarity, eliminating telco contract anxiety through bold plan value propositions, transparent pricing tiers, and immediate visual feedback.
- **Visual Mood:** High-contrast blue tones convey infrastructure resilience and signal quality, balanced by high-energy orange accents that drive user forward-momentum through the acquisition funnel.

## Colors

The color palette establishes high visual contrast, meeting WCAG 2.1 AAA standards across textual elements and AA standards across touch targets.

- **Primary Blue (`#005C9E`):** Extracted directly from brand identity. Represents network reliability, data clarity, and structure. Used for primary branding elements, high-emphasis icons, active tab underlines, and key narrative anchor points.
- **Neutral Dark Navy (`#0B1B3B`):** Replaces pure black to deliver deep, editorial-grade contrast for typography, prominent headings, and high-impact dark cards (e.g., premium promo banners or sticky mobile carts).
- **Secondary Light Background (`#F8FAFC` & `#FFFFFF`):** Provides a bright, spacious, and breathable atmosphere that reduces clutter in multi-tiered plan comparisons.
- **Tertiary Energetic Orange (`#FF6A13`):** Reserved exclusively as a high-conversion trigger. Strictly applied to primary action buttons (`Pide tu chip`, `Cámbiate ya`), conversion badges (`50% Dscto.`), and active focus rings.
- **Named Functional Tones:**
  - `accent-orange-soft` (`#FFF4ED`): Gentle container fill for promotional tags and step highlights without competing against the solid orange CTA buttons.
  - `border-subtle` (`#E2E8F0`): Clean container delimiters for plan grids and input frames.

## Typography

The type system is powered by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, offering a balance of geometric precision and friendly humanist terminals. It mirrors the geometric clarity found in modern telecommunications branding while maintaining legibility in numerical data and dense plan specification tables.

### Rules of Usage
- **Price Displays:** Utilize `plan-price-lg` with tight negative tracking (`-0.03em`) to anchor plan cards. Currency symbols (`S/`) and period intervals (`/mes`) should be aligned using `label-md` or `headline-sm` baselines.
- **Headlines:** Use tight letter spacing for display levels to ensure punchy, commanding sales messaging without awkward line breaks.
- **Labels & CTAs:** Keep button text strictly set to `label-lg` or `label-md` in semi-bold/bold weights to guarantee rapid scannability on handheld screens.

## Layout & Spacing

A responsive 12-column grid layout serves desktop environments, collapsing into 6 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns, max-width `1280px`, centered, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 6 columns, `margin: 1.5rem`, `gutter: 1rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin: 1rem`, `gutter: 0.75rem`.

### Vertical Flow & Spatial Rhythm
- Apply `space-xl` (2.5rem) between major content segments and card grid systems.
- Section paddings utilize generous vertical blocks (`4rem` to `6rem`) on desktop to establish an uncluttered, premium feel, condensing down to `2.5rem` on mobile.
- Form controls and plan feature lists use strict `space-sm` (8px) and `space-md` (16px) element gaps for consistent vertical rhythm.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages **Ambient Shadows** and **Tonal Layering**, rejecting harsh, artificial black drop-shadows in favor of soft, navy-tinted ambient diffusions.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat):** Base canvas in `#F8FAFC` or `#FFFFFF`. Subtle `1px solid #E2E8F0` border demarcation for standard containers.
- **Level 1 (Card Rest):** Used for non-featured plan cards, device listings, and FAQ items.
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(11, 27, 59, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(11, 27, 59, 0.03);`
  Border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Selected):** Interactive lift on hover and default state for the "Recommended / Más Vendido" portabilidad plan card.
  `box-shadow: 0 16px 32px -6px rgba(0, 92, 158, 0.12), 0 6px 12px -2px rgba(11, 27, 59, 0.04);`
  Border: `2px solid #005C9E`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Controls / Overlays):** Sticky bottom portabilidad conversion bars and checkout modals.
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -8px rgba(11, 27, 59, 0.18);`

## Shapes

The design uses confident, approachable curvatures that reflect the friendly circular glyph in the corporate identity.

- **Primary Geometry:** Base `roundedness: 2` delivers `0.5rem` (8px) on standard inputs, checkboxes, and compact chips.
- **Cards & Plan Modules:** Standardize on `rounded-xl` (`1rem` / 16px) and `rounded-2xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px) for parent packaging. The generous radius softens the technical density of gigabytes, minutes, and legal disclaimers.
- **Buttons & Pills:** Interactive buttons and discount badges use full-pill styling (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to invite clicks and distinguish themselves from angular structural containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA (`Tertiary Orange`):** Background `#FF6A13`, text `#FFFFFF`, font-weight bold, `border-radius: 9999px`. Subtle transition on hover (`background-color: #E55606`, upward transform: `-1px`). Used strictly for decisive conversion steps.
- **Secondary CTA (`Brand Blue Outline`):** Background transparent, border `2px solid #005C9E`, text `#005C9E`, `border-radius: 9999px`. Hover fills background with `#F0F7FF`.
- **Tertiary Ghost Button:** Transparent background, text `#005C9E`, underlined on hover. Used for auxiliary links like "Ver términos y condiciones".

### Plan Comparison Cards (Core Component)
- **Container:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `1.5rem`, padding `2rem`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Featured / Best Seller Modifier:** Border `2px solid #005C9E`, elevation Level 2. Position an absolute pill badge at top center (`background: #FF6A13`, text white, `label-sm`).
- **Data Callout:** Massive numeric display (`plan-price-lg`), color `#0B1B3B`, paired with gigabyte counters in bold `#005C9E`.
- **Benefits Checklist:** Left-aligned check icons tinted with `#005C9E` or `#16A34A` encased in soft circular backdrops (`#F0F7FF`), accompanied by `body-sm` typography.

### Input Fields & Verification Flow
- **Standard Field:** Height `52px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E2E8F0`, corner radius `0.75rem`, text `#0B1B3B`.
- **Active / Focus:** Border `2px solid #005C9E`, box shadow `0 0 0 4px rgba(0, 92, 158, 0.15)`, background `#FFFFFF`.
- **Input with Inline Action:** Ideal for entering the line to port ("Ingresa tu número a portar") with an inline orange submission pill button pinned to the right edge.

### Chips & Filter Toggles
- **Plan Switcher (Postpago vs. Chip Suelto vs. Equipos):** Segmented controller with container background `#F1F5F9`, internal padding `4px`, radius `9999px`. Active segment receives solid `#FFFFFF` background, navy text `#0B1B3B`, and Level 1 shadow.

### Floating Mobile Cart / Portability Bar
- **Sticky Footer Component:** Fixed to mobile bottom viewport. White background with Level 3 shadow, displays currently selected plan, discounted monthly sum in `#0B1B3B`, and a full-width or inline `#FF6A13` CTA button.
